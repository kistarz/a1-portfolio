Cyber@UCI Merchandise

Designing CYBER@UCI's merchandise was a process of exploration, experimentation, and refinement. From initial sketches and brainstorming sessions to countless design iterations, each step helped to discover how to best represent the community’s identity and creativity. Throughout this process, I learned how important brand identity is in creating designs that feel cohesive, recognizable, and meaningful. I also gained a deeper appreciation for how typography, color, and visual elements work together to communicate a brand’s personality. Although the process came with challenges and revisions, seeing ideas come together as a cohesive collection made every step worthwhile.

Ideation

Each merch design begins with a process of sketching and ideation, where we explore different concepts, visual styles, and creative directions. Through rough sketches and inspiration boards, we experiment with colors, typography, and graphic elements before refining our ideas into cohesive designs.

Draft Iteration

During the draft iteration phase, we explored different logo styles, typography, and graphic concepts for CYBER@UCI merchandise. Through multiple sketches and design variations, we experimented with incorporating our branding into different visuals, refining each concept to better reflect our identity and establish a cohesive look across our merchandise.

Final Look!

Our final merchandise collection brings CYBER@UCI’s visual identity to life through a cohesive range of T-shirts and hoodies. Combining bold typography, vibrant blue graphics, and playful illustrations, each piece reflects our community’s creativity and passion for technology. These final designs are ready to represent CYBER@UCI both on and off campus!
