3D Server Rack

Designing an interactive 3D server rack was one of my first experiences exploring 3D modeling. Using Spline, I learned how to build and customize the server’s components while experimenting with lighting, materials, and interactive elements. Although learning a new tool came with challenges, the process pushed me outside my comfort zone and helped me discover new ways to bring my designs to life beyond a flat screen.
